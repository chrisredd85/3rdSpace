Spatial progress

Replace numbered dot steppers with a single luminous progress line. Users feel motion — not counting. The glowing terminus tells them exactly where they are without a label.

🫧

Breathing room

Every element needs air. Labels are uppercase micro-type at 11px/600. Inputs are 48px tall. Each step contains only what is needed, nothing more.

✦

Tactile chips

Multi-select choices become pill chips with a crisp activated state — indigo tint + glow. No checkboxes. No nested lists. The selection feels like picking physical objects.

🌑

Deep black canvas

Pure void (#0a0a0f) background with 4–8% white glass cards. Inputs are 1px bordered rectangles — minimal until focused, then they bloom with an indigo halo.

⬜

White CTA button

"Continue" is always pure white on void — the highest contrast possible. The final "publish" action uses the brand gradient as reward. Back buttons are ghost-minimal.

🔀

Conditional surfaces

The Venue's bar-pricing panel and Vendor's emergency-rate field appear as tinted sub-panels only when toggled — no layout shift, content breathes in from zero opacity.

Design decisions — Creator path

The 4-step flow is unchanged in data but reordered for cognition: Identity first (lowest friction), then org context (they feel known), then event type + amenities (feels like personalization, not a form), then ticketing as the final reward — connecting Eventbrite is exciting, not admin. The bulk-booking toggle replaces a checkbox with a tactile switch card.

Design decisions — Venue path

The most data-dense path (5 steps) is tamed by grouping around what the venue owner cares about — not what the database needs. The bar-pricing panel conditionally reveals only when toggled, using a violet tint to signal it's a separate revenue "module." The availability step ends with a calm green sync card — a deliberately low-anxiety close before publish.

Design decisions — Vendor path

Vendors are often creatives who care deeply about first impressions. The bio and portfolio fields are elevated early. Pricing is grouped as a "package builder" card — framing it as a creative product, not a rate card. The emergency-vendor toggle uses an amber tint to signal urgency and opportunity simultaneously. Clicking the entire panel activates it — no tiny checkbox hunting.
